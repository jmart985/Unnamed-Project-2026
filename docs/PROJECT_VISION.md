# Project vision

## The long-term game

Survive and build a life in a simulated world where people, creatures, settlements, and factions interact and create unscripted stories. The player chooses an identity: traveler, trader, builder, rescuer, breeder, mercenary, or another role supported by the world.

Kenshi inspires freedom and lasting consequences; RimWorld inspires needs, jobs, and colony stories; Palworld inspires creatures participating in work and daily life. These are design references, not asset or content sources.

People and creatures inhabit the same world. Creatures may eventually have diets, anatomy, temperaments, needs, memories, relationships, and work capabilities. Capture, captivity, bonding, recruitment, and domestication may share infrastructure while retaining meaningful behavioral differences. None of those future systems is required for the initial sandbox.

The intended scales are individual, squad, settlement, and world. We will earn each larger scale by proving the smaller one first.

## What we are proving now

An entity becomes hungry, finds accessible food, travels to it, consumes a real item, and returns to useful activity. Humans haul wood. Creatures wander and eat using the same foundations. Saving and loading preserves durable state without duplicating resources.

The Ant Farm foundation is the completed baseline. Current work should follow the active GitHub milestone and its concrete implementation issues, expanding the simulation in small, testable increments. Success means the behavior is observable, explainable, and repeatable enough to improve.

See [AI contributor workflow](AI_WORKFLOW.md) and [the backlog](BACKLOG.md) for current handoff rules. Milestone 0.1 remains a regression reference, not a permanent scope ceiling.

## Development Philosophy

Prototype first. Replace placeholder systems only after gameplay proves they are valuable.
