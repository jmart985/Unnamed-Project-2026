# Playtest village homes

Start a **new game** to create ten resident homes on a roughly 44 m radius ring. Each has an inward-facing entrance, an interior rest destination and a bed. Pitched roofs, timber trim, windows, varied plaster colours and connecting footpaths replace the sixteen solid placeholder blocks. The workbench and farm remain world-owned in their existing positions. The crossroads is clear and the two main lanes are 4 m wide so houses remain off the road.

Houses use an **8 x 8 m footprint**, twice the old width and depth. Doors have **2.4 m clear width and 2.6 m clear height**; floors finish flush with the ground and ceilings are above the supported player capsule. Build mode previews, construction sites and placement checks use the enlarged dimensions. Completed floors, ceilings, walls and closed doors are solid. `HouseGeometry` defines physical parts in metres; Godot rotates them by saved quarter turns and expands navigation wall exclusions by 0.35 m. NPC routes enter through doors and do not treat entire rooms as blocked cells. Interaction range is measured from the doorway rather than the house center.

Residents keep existing employment and schedules. During rest, capable residents navigate inside, clear the threshold, then close and lock their own door before settling at the interior home destination. They unlock and open it when leaving for work or outside food. Food-service door approaches operate during both work and rest, preserve the food candidate, and yield to higher-priority threats. Food already inside needs no exit. Follow and squad movement route via the owner's handle without changing order intent. Door approaches use a tighter arrival tolerance than ordinary home arrival so actors cannot stop just short of a usable handle.

World validates door operations. Only the assigned resident can explicitly lock or unlock a home, and a locked door denies opening from outside by other actors. A capable visitor already inside may use the interior latch to leave. Open/close/lock/unlock operations are available through semantic interactions and preserve capability/range checks. Doors cannot remain locked while open or ownerless. Insufficient storage allows home return with cargo held safely; only when capacity for the entire carried stack becomes available off shift does delivery cancel the home trip. A smaller gap does not interrupt rest. Released workers may finish their existing cargo at an eligible stockpile without regaining employment. Scheduled rest currently means resting at the interior destination; autonomous bed sleeping and sleep animations are not implemented by this change.

After door geometry changes, movement and AI service polls wait for a newer NavigationServer map iteration and a refreshed agent path. This prevents an order from being marked unreachable against the cached closed-door route; a fixed frame delay alone is insufficient. Changing only the lock label does not rebuild the navigation mesh. Crowd avoidance also reapplies squad and individual Follow clearance to redirected steps before accepting them.

New playable villages use a **two-minute day** at normal simulation speed. The default 08:00–18:00 worker finishes its shift after 50 seconds and returns to work at 120 seconds. Other residents keep their varied day/night schedules. This changes calendar progression only: movement, hunger and other simulation rates are unchanged. Pause and simulation speed controls continue to work. The status display shows day duration alongside time; the inspector's transition countdown uses actual simulation seconds.

Schema 28 saves resident references, lock state, house orientation, village creation mode and day duration. Earlier snapshots without lock state default to unlocked. Loading restores these fields; reset preserves village mode and day duration. Saves from schema 27 and earlier retain their four-minute clock and customized settlement/home positions. They are not silently relocated into the ring. Start a new game for the complete new layout. Legacy unfinished 4 x 4 starter huts migrate to 8 x 8 construction blueprints. Removing a resident unlocks the vacant house. Invalid facing, resident references, duplicate home ownership, impossible locks or clock values reject before changing the live world or cancelling transient paths.

## Verification

`dotnet test UnnamedProject2026.slnx --configuration Release` covers layout spacing, inward facing, scale/footprint overlap, schedule timing, full-storage rest, door approach, vacant houses, snapshot round trips and invalid-load atomicity.

`--validate-village` is registered in the PR validation manifest. It starts through the actual character-menu path, moves the largest male/female player capsules into and out of each cardinal door orientation, checks a closed door and reopening from outside, routes all ten real NPC views through their doorways both ways, and runs a natural two-minute schedule cycle. Employed residents resume work; residents without an eligible workplace resume their ordinary activity. It checks saved locks, actual hungry exits/eating in work and rest windows, privacy and interior-latch interactions, resuming off-shift delivery when capacity becomes available, squad/Follow exit routes, and save/load/reset. It does not infer traversal from an anchor name or an approach-only route.

For rendered evidence, run the same scenario with `--capture-village` using a rendering display driver. It saves an overview and house close-up under `docs/evidence/village/`, then exits without claiming a validation pass. This optional capture path is separate from the headless acceptance run.
